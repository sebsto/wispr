🎤 Introducing Wispr – Privacy-First Voice Dictation for macOS

Tired of typing? Let your voice do the work.

Wispr is a free, open-source voice dictation app that turns your speech into text instantly – all while keeping your privacy intact. Unlike cloud-based solutions, Wispr processes everything locally on your Mac using on-device AI.

✨ Key Features:
• Global hotkey (Option+Space) – dictate anywhere, in any app
• 100% private – no audio ever leaves your device
• Lightning fast – powered by Whisper AI
• 97 languages supported with auto-detection

Perfect for writers, developers, students, and anyone who wants to work faster without compromising privacy.

🔗 Try it now: https://wispr.stormacq.com

Install via Homebrew:
```
brew tap sebsto/macos

```

## Mastodon (500 characters)

A month ago I shipped a macOS dictation app built in a day. Since then: 19 releases, 70+ stars, and real users shaping the roadmap.

Lessons learned:
• The Fn key needs its own event system (Carbon won't do it)
• Sharing ML models across sandbox boundaries is tricky
• Parakeet V3 is faster than Whisper for European languages
• Ship early, iterate on real feedback
