Welcome to Wispr

On-device speech-to-text transcription for your Mac.

Wispr is a menu bar app that turns your voice into text using OpenAI Whisper and NVIDIA Parakeet models — running entirely on your Mac.

  • Hotkey-triggered dictation — press a shortcut to record; transcribed text is inserted at your cursor.
  • Dual engine architecture — choose between OpenAI Whisper and NVIDIA Parakeet through one unified interface.
  • Low-latency streaming — Parakeet Realtime provides end-of-utterance detection for near-instant results.
  • Multi-language support — Whisper handles 90+ languages, Parakeet V3 handles 25.
  • Menu bar native — lives in your menu bar and stays out of the way.

Your audio never leaves your Mac. All transcription happens on-device. The network is used only to download models and check for updates.
