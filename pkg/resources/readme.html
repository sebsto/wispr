Before You Install

System Requirements

  • macOS 26.2 or later
  • Apple Silicon Mac (arm64) — Intel Macs are not supported
  • A microphone (built-in or external)

What Gets Installed

  • Wispr.app is installed into /Applications.
  • The app bundles the optional WisprCLI command-line tool at Contents/Resources/bin/WisprCLI, which transcribes audio and video files offline from the terminal.

After Installing

  1. Launch Wispr from /Applications. It lives in the menu bar — there is no Dock icon.
  2. Follow the onboarding flow to grant Microphone and Accessibility permissions.
  3. Download a model on first launch (Whisper Tiny is ~75 MB; larger models offer higher accuracy). Models download over the network as an explicit action.
  4. Choose a dictation hotkey and try a test dictation.

Your audio never leaves your Mac — all transcription runs on-device.
